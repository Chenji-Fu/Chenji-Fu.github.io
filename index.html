I am a PhD student at the University of Bonn under the supervision of Jessica Fintzen and Peter Scholze.

Research interests

I'm interested in algebraic geometry, number theory and representation theory.

More precisely, I'm interested in the Langlands program, representations of finite reductive groups and p-adic groups, and the shtuka spaces.
